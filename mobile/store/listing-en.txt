مرصود – Marsoud

SHORT DESCRIPTION (max 80)
Employee app for Marsoud: attendance, tasks, leads, meetings and requests.

FULL DESCRIPTION (max 4000)
Marsoud is a complete business management platform for companies. This app is the employee side of it — everything you need from your working day, on your phone.

Note: the app interface is in Arabic (right-to-left).

⏱ ATTENDANCE
Clock in and out from your phone with location capture, and review your full monthly record with the hours worked each day.

✅ TASKS
Every task you own or are part of, grouped by status — new, in progress, review, done, blocked — with its priority and due date.

🎯 LEADS
Track your leads through every stage: new, contacted, meeting scheduled, negotiation, proposal sent — with contact details and the expected value of each opportunity.

📅 MEETINGS AND SCHEDULE
Your upcoming meetings and recurring commitments in one place, and you can add a new meeting from the app.

📮 REQUESTS
Submit leave, advance and permission requests, and follow their status as they are reviewed.

📝 DAILY REPORTS
Write your daily report and send it to your manager directly from the app.

💵 CUSTODY
Track the cash and items in your custody, and request new custody.

📁 FILES AND ARCHIVE
Your documents and attachments, always available.

🔔 INSTANT NOTIFICATIONS
Get notified the moment a task is assigned to you, a meeting is coming up, or one of your leads is updated.

—

This app requires a Marsoud employee account issued by your company. If you do not have an account, contact the HR department where you work.
